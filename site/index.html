How it works

You add the meeting. Meira handles the rest.

Put it in your calendar

As you already do. Meira picks up new events on her own, including the ones a recruiter sends with the address hidden in the invitation text.

She works backwards

From the start time she counts back through the trip, the time you need to get ready and anything you should prepare, all the way to your alarm.

She gets you out the door

The alarm rings, the "leave now" arrives on time, and if you're still home when the bus is close, she tells you that too.
